The following site consists of my notes from the text "C++ Primer," by Barbara E. Moo, Josée Lajoie, and Stanley B. Lippman. Furthermore, I intend to use this site to collect tidbits, tips, and direct summaries which I believe to
expedite the learning outcome of a reader who might intend to use the notes here for revision purposes. I may not have left much explanation on what type of code I am writing, so essentially, <b>this site serves to be an 
enclylopedia</b> for revision of C++ under its main objective and not a new learning place.
